<h1 align="center">🛡️ Armor Cosmetic</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Wear two sets of armor — one for function, one for fashion.</strong></p>

<br>

---

<br>

<h2>✨ Overview</h2>

<p>Armor Cosmetic lets you equip a separate cosmetic armor set that displays over your real armor. Keep your best protective gear equipped while showing off any look you want — without sacrificing stats.</p>

<br>

<h2>🎯 Features</h2>

<h3>🎭 Dual Armor System</h3>
<p>Wear one armor set for protection stats, another purely for display. Your real armor stays equipped underneath.</p>

<h3>🎛️ Cosmetic Inventory GUI</h3>
<p>Open the cosmetic armor screen directly from your normal inventory with a single button click.</p>

<h3>👕 Per-Slot Toggles</h3>
<p>Each cosmetic slot has an individual toggle (S/A) that hides your <strong>real armor</strong> for that slot. Toggle ON to show your skin or cosmetic armor underneath.</p>

<h3>🔄 Global Toggle</h3>
<p>Disable all cosmetic rendering with one click — useful for PvP or when you want to see your real armor.</p>

<h3>⚰️ Corail Tombstone Compatible</h3>
<p>Cosmetic items are captured by tombstones when you die. No more losing your fashion to the void.</p>

<h3>🌍 11 Languages</h3>
<p>English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Turkish, Czech.</p>

<h3>⚙️ Fully Configurable</h3>
<p>All button positions, sizes, and visibility can be customized in the config file.</p>

<h3>💻 Commands</h3>
<p><code>/clearcosarmor</code> — Clear cosmetic armor from players.<br>
<code>/coshat</code> — Toggle cosmetic helmet visibility.</p>

<br>

<br>

<h2>🎮 How to Use</h2>

<ol>
<li>Open your inventory and click the cosmetic armor button (📦) to open the cosmetic GUI.</li>
<li>Place cosmetic armor items in the cosmetic slots.</li>
<li>Use the <strong>S/A toggles</strong> above each slot to show or hide your real armor for that piece.</li>
<li>Use the <strong>global toggle</strong> button to disable all cosmetic rendering at once.</li>
</ol>
