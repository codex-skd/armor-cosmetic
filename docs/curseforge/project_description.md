# 🛡️ ArmorCosmetic

**Wear two sets of armor — one for function, one for fashion.**

ArmorCosmetic lets you equip a separate cosmetic armor set that displays over your real armor. Keep your best protective gear equipped while showing off any look you want.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎭 Dual Armor System** — Wear one armor set for stats, another for display.
- **🎛️ Cosmetic Inventory GUI** — Access from the normal inventory screen via a button.
- **👕 Per-Slot Toggles** — Hide individual pieces of your **real armor** to show your skin underneath.
- **🔄 Global Toggle** — Disable all cosmetic rendering with one click (useful for PvP).
- **⚰️ Corail Tombstone Compatible** — Cosmetic items are captured by tombstones when you die.
- **🌍 11 Languages** — English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Turkish, Czech.
- **⚙️ Fully Configurable** — Button positions, sizes, and visibility in the config file.
- **💻 Commands** — `/clearcosarmor`, `/coshat`.

---

## 📋 Requirements

- Minecraft **26.1.2**
- NeoForge **26.1.2.76+**

---

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open your inventory and click the **cosmetic armor button** (📦) to open the cosmetic GUI.
2. Place cosmetic armor items in the cosmetic slots.
3. Use the **S/A toggles** above each slot to show/hide your real armor.
4. Use the **global toggle** button to disable all cosmetic rendering.
