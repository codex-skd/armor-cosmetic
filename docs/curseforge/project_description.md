<h1 align="center">✨ Armor Cosmetic</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Wear two sets of armor — one for function, one for fashion.</strong></p>

<br>

---

<br>

<h2>✨ Overview</h2>

<p>Armor Cosmetic lets you equip a separate cosmetic armor set that displays over your real armor, keeping your best protective gear equipped while showing off any look you want. A fork of <strong>CosmeticArmorReworked</strong> by <em>zlainsama</em> and <em>dmillerw</em>, ported to NeoForge. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the original authors.</p>

<br>

<h2>🎯 Features</h2>

<h3>🛡️ Dual Armor System</h3>
<p>Wear one armor set for protection stats, another purely for display. Your real armor stays equipped underneath — you get the stats of your netherite while looking like you're wearing leather.</p>

<h3>🖥️ Cosmetic Inventory GUI</h3>
<p>Open the cosmetic armor screen from your normal inventory with a single button click. Manage all cosmetic slots in a familiar interface.</p>

<h3>🔘 Per-Slot Toggles</h3>
<p>Each cosmetic slot has an S/A toggle that hides your real armor for that slot. Show off your cosmetic helmet while keeping your real chestplate visible.</p>

<h3>🌐 Global Toggle</h3>
<p>Disable all cosmetic rendering with one click — useful for PvP or when you want to see your actual armor.</p>

<h3>⚰️ Corail Tombstone Compatible</h3>
<p>Cosmetic items are captured by tombstones when you die, so you won't lose your fashion.</p>

<h3>🌍 15 Languages</h3>
<p>English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Turkish, Czech.</p>

<h3>⚙️ Fully Configurable</h3>
<p>Button positions, sizes, and visibility can be customized in the config file.</p>

<h3>💻 Commands</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>/clearcosarmor</code> — Clear cosmetic armor from players</li>
<li><code>/coshat</code> — Toggle cosmetic helmet visibility</li>
</ul>

<h2>🎮 How to Use</h2>

<ol>
<li>Install the mod on <strong>both client and server</strong>.</li>
<li>Open your inventory and click the new cosmetic armor button.</li>
<li>Place cosmetic armor pieces in the slots — they render over your real armor.</li>
<li>Use S/A toggles per slot to control what shows.</li>
<li>Use the global toggle to disable cosmetic rendering entirely.</li>
</ol>
