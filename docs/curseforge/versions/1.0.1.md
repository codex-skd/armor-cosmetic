<h2>v1.0.1 - Corail Tombstone compatibility fix</h2>

<h3>Fixed</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Corail Tombstone</strong>: dying with Tombstone installed no longer scatters your cosmetic armor. Previously the cosmetic pieces were dropped into the grave, restored to the normal inventory on recovery, and auto-equipped into the real armor slots, leaving the cosmetic slots empty. When Tombstone is present, the cosmetic armor inventory is now kept intact through death and stays in its cosmetic slots after respawn.</li>
</ul>
