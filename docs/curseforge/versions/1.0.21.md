<h2>v1.0.21 - Tombstone Compatibility: Real Armor Captured</h2>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Real armor lost on death with Tombstone</strong>: The player&#8217;s real armor is now added to <code>LivingDropsEvent</code> alongside cosmetic armor. This ensures Tombstone captures <strong>both</strong> the real and cosmetic armor into the grave.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technical Changes</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>InventoryManager.handlePlayerDrops()</code> now iterates the player&#8217;s armor slots (head, chest, legs, feet) and adds those items to <code>event.getDrops()</code>.</li>
<li>Real armor items are <strong>cleared</strong> from the player inventory after being added to drops, so no duplication occurs.</li>
<li>This guarantees compatibility with Tombstone regardless of how it internally handles drops.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Server owners: update to this version to ensure your players&#8217; armor is always saved by Tombstone on death.</blockquote>
