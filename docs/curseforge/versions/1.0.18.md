## v1.0.18 — Per-Slot Toggle Fix: Hide Real Armor, Show Skin

The individual slot toggles (S/A buttons above each cosmetic slot) now hide the **real armor** instead of the cosmetic armor.

- **Toggle ON (green/S)** = hides your real armor → shows just your skin (or cosmetic armor if equipped).
- **Toggle OFF (gray/A)** = normal behavior (shows real armor, with cosmetic on top if equipped).

Now you can wear functional armor underneath while displaying only your skin or cosmetic armor.
