# ArmorCosmetic

**Wear two sets of armor — one for function, one for fashion.**

ArmorCosmetic lets you equip a separate cosmetic armor set that displays over your real armor. Keep your best protective gear equipped while showing off any look you want.

## Features

- **Dual armor system**: Wear one armor set for stats, another for display.
- **Cosmetic inventory GUI**: Access from the normal inventory screen via a button.
- **Per-slot toggles**: Hide individual pieces of your **real armor** to show your skin underneath (useful for mixing skin + armor looks).
- **Global toggle**: Disable all cosmetic rendering with one click (for PvP).
- **Corail Tombstone compatible**: Cosmetic items are captured by tombstones when you die.
- **11 languages**: English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Turkish, Czech.
- **Fully configurable**: Button positions, sizes, and visibility in the config file.
- **Commands**: `/clearcosarmor`, `/coshat`.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.1.2
- NeoForge 26.1.2.76+
